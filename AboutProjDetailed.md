## 📖 About

LifeQuest is a full-stack web application that helps friend groups create, track, and celebrate their shared and individual life goals together. Instead of maintaining scattered personal to-do lists, users can form groups, add bucket list items — ranging from travel destinations and adventure activities to skills they want to learn — and track progress collaboratively.

Each item moves through a clear lifecycle (**Not Started → In Progress → Completed**), letting users document their journey with notes and updates along the way. Upon completion, users can upload photos and share their achievement on a group **"Wall of Fame,"** turning personal milestones into shared memories the whole group can celebrate through reactions and comments.

The platform also supports group challenges, deadline reminders, a leaderboard highlighting the most active members, and an optional **year-end recap** that summarizes each user's accomplishments as a shareable snapshot.

By combining goal-setting, social accountability, and celebration into one platform, LifeQuest aims to make personal aspirations more achievable — and more meaningful — when pursued alongside friends.

## ✨ Planned Features _(indicative, not final)_

- **User & Group Management** — auth, multi-group membership, admin/member roles, invite via link/code
- **Bucket List Items** — categorized goals with descriptions, deadlines, priority tags, and visibility control
- **Progress Tracking** — status lifecycle with notes and change history
- **Completion & Celebration** — proof uploads and a group-wide "Wall of Fame"
- **Social Interaction** — reactions, comments, and friend tagging on completed items
- **Group Challenges** — shared goals with individual contribution tracking
- **Notifications & Reminders** — deadline alerts, completion notifications, invite alerts
- **Leaderboard & Gamification** — activity-based rankings and achievement badges
- **Year-End Recap** — auto-generated, shareable summary of the year's achievements
- **Search & Privacy Controls** — filter/search items, public/private visibility for items and groups
